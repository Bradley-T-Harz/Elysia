# Fusion F3D/F3Z boundary

F3D and F3Z support is intentionally limited and honest. Elysia identifies and hashes the selected file and reports bounded basic metadata. For F3Z it recognizes the ZIP-compatible container, applies archive-style entry and size limits, and summarizes contained references without extraction or loading. F3D remains opaque metadata-only.

For local-first geometry work, export a neutral STEP, STL, or DXF derivative from the trusted authoring environment and inspect that derivative. Elysia does not promise full Fusion parsing, require an Autodesk account, invoke Fusion, use Autodesk translation, upload source data, or contact a cloud service.

Any future Autodesk integration would be optional and revocable, require explicit account authorization and a clear warning that data may leave local control, and remain outside Elysia's sacred local core. No such integration is present now.

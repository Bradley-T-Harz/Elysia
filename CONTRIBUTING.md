# Contributing

Contributions must be original or accompanied by complete, compatible provenance and license evidence. Do not submit secrets, private source, personal data, model weights, generated media of uncertain origin, copied snippets without permission, or material that cannot be redistributed.

By submitting a contribution, you represent that you have the right to provide it and license the contribution under Apache-2.0, except where a maintainer has explicitly approved a separately licensed directory by name. Preserve existing copyright, license, attribution, safety, privacy, and capability boundaries. Submission does not grant trademark rights or guarantee acceptance.
